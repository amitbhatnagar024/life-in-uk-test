# life-in-uk-test

Free study guide and practice questions for the **Life in the UK test**.

- **`study.html`** — a condensed study guide covering everything in the official handbook (values, history, modern society, government, law). Designed to be read in a few hours or a single day, with a one-day study plan, a progress tracker and a last-hour cheat sheet.
- **`index.html`** — timed practice tests in the real format: 24 questions, 45 minutes, 18 correct to pass. 531 questions covering every chapter of the handbook, split into 22 fixed tests plus a random test that draws a fresh 24 each time. Includes single-answer, true/false and "choose two" questions. Each answer's explanation appears only after you choose an option.

Recommended: read the study guide first, then take the practice tests until you're scoring 20+ out of 24.

Based on *Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents* (3rd edition). Not affiliated with the Home Office — book the real test at [gov.uk/life-in-the-uk-test](https://www.gov.uk/life-in-the-uk-test).
